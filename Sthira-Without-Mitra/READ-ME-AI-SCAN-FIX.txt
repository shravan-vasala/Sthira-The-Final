STHIRA - AI SERVICE RECOVERY FIX
===============================

WHAT THE NEW SCREENSHOTS SHOW
The app now reaches Gemini. Google returned HTTP 503 / UNAVAILABLE on
both configured photo models. Four attempts consumed the scan budget;
the last attempt had under one second and ended with a timeout.

WHAT THIS UPDATE CHANGES
- A busy photo model without a provider cooldown gets one attempt before
  switching to the configured fallback, after a one-second backoff.
- If the fallback is busy too, stop and show the service-busy message.
- Do not start a follow-up with less than four seconds of useful time.
- Reserve 250 ms to return the result before the outer scan deadline.
- If local recovery time runs out after a known provider-busy response,
  keep that useful error. Later explicit provider errors still take priority.
- Keep the 30-second overall limit, models, nutrition validation, provider
  cooldowns, quota handling and cancellation.

This improves recovery and avoids needless waiting. It cannot guarantee
a successful scan while Google's models are unavailable. Manual meal
entry remains available. Live Gemini access on your device is not tested.
Provider guidance: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/troubleshooting

ALSO INCLUDED FROM THE PREVIOUS FIX
- Typed image request parts: prevents the old pre-network Dart TypeError.
- Daily Check-in collection syntax compatible with the Isar 3 generator.
- Isar model-only generation and removal of the unused Riverpod builder.
